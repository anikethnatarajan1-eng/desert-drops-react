Questions and answers

Explore common questions and clear answers about our tools, data, and impact. Search, filter, and listen — built for real users.

Water Calculator

How does the water calculator work?

It estimates weekly usage from showers, laundry, and garden inputs; applies regional flow rates; and shows a breakdown with tips.

Conservation Map

What is the Conservation Map used for?

It highlights restoration projects, water quality zones, and community efforts. Filter by region, type, or impact to connect local actions to global change.

NASA Weather

Where does the weather data come from?

Satellite feeds and models via NASA and Open‑Meteo. We geocode city names and fetch current conditions — fast, free, and reliable.

Accessibility

What makes these tools accessible?

Semantic structure, keyboard parity, captions/transcripts, bilingual content, and device‑level performance tuning.
