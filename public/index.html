Why this matters

Maps shape narratives. Narratives shape policy. Policy shapes lives. Desert Drops builds tools that listen—to residents, histories, and futures—and turns them into practical pathways for equity.

Community‑first design

Center lived experience and local expertise. Let residents define success, then measure toward it.

Transparent storytelling

Illuminate inequities without sensationalizing—and highlight solutions with humility and rigor.

Impact over optics

Focus on outcomes that shift conditions on the ground, not vanity metrics or performative dashboards.
